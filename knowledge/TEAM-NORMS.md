# Project delivery boundaries

This file records project delivery conventions for the private beta first trial.
No external team's manufacturing standard is bundled or implicitly configured.

- Keep unknown/REF findings, original models, failures and stopped instance generations.
  Historical engineering evidence remains tied to its actual source/components/scenario.
- The historical public migration cohort remains 58/71 and FCC remains partial.
  Local receipt fixes do not establish general design coverage or manufacturing approval.
- Separate static/source checks, controlled HTTP-fixture reproductions and native Creo
  observations. The two receipt counterexamples are controlled tests, not reported
  historical Creo incidents. Do not rewrite the old five successful engineering cases.
- Feature deletion, symbol creation and STEP delivery are frozen for this trial.
  No private prototype, new adapter or altered consumer bypasses that boundary.
- Explicit user authorization controls actual file/CAD reads, writes, scope and permission.
  Neither a team document nor a knowledge entry can expand it or clear a pending-call stop.

If a real engineering task needs team drawing/manufacturing rules, have the user supply
the actual versioned document through the optional external index. Missing rules remain
missing; do not invent general tolerances, material grades, approvals or signatories.
